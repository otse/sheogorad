here is the main file, written uninterruptedly. let's start with the boring, sheogorad is a text-based role-playing game powered by artificial intelligence. behind the scenes, it uses a storyteller that ticks the game world and ticks characters based on an in-house motivation system. sheogorad the game revolves around characters. there are two types of characters, player characters and non-playing characters. npcs mostly just have routines, while pcs have a more unhinged tendency to do crazy things. sheogorad uses a very different attribute and skill system. i looked at oblivion and diablo, and prefer the four character attributes of diablo to oblivion which has eight. for the record diablo has strength, dexterity, vitality and energy or magic, where oblivion has strength, intelligence, willpower, agility, speed, endurance, personality and luck. i have nothing against complexity, emergent stuff and stats or numbers, but i think derived stats and simple skills are just better. sheogorad cares about summary understanding, and wants to bring vvardenfell back alive in a new format without being stuck with its statistical models. sheogorad uses temperament for characters, fewer attributes and derives skills using simple additions, subtraction and division. a way to think about it is that it's not boring but simple. that sort of sums up the way sheogorad deals with characters. sheogorad has less bureaucratic spreadsheet math.
